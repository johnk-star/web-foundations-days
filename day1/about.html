About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you organize thoughts and boost daily productivity seamlessly. It offers a distraction-free environment for quickly capturing ideas and keeping track of important tasks.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note or task into the input field on the home page.
  2. Click the submit button or press Enter to add the note to your list.
  3. Review, edit, or clear your notes whenever you need to stay organized.

Features

  • Fast and responsive text entry
  • Simple, uncluttered interface
  • Keyboard shortcut support for power users

Keyboard Shortcuts
